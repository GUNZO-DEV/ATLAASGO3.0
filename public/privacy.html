Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

AtlaasGo (“we”, “us”) operates the AtlaasGo mobile app and website, an on-demand delivery platform for food, groceries, and pharmacy items in Ifrane, Morocco. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using AtlaasGo you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address, and phone number, used to create and secure your account (authentication is handled by our provider, Clerk).
  • Delivery location & addresses — GPS location and the landmarks/addresses you save, so riders can reach your exact spot.
  • Orders & activity — items ordered, order history, favorites, wallet balance and transactions, and in-app messages related to a delivery.
  • Payment information — card payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your full card number on our servers.
  • Device & usage data — basic technical data and notifications tokens needed to operate the app and send order updates.
  • Support messages — anything you send us at support@atlaasgo.com.

How we use your information

  • To take, prepare, assign, and deliver your orders.
  • To process payments and manage your wallet and any membership.
  • To match orders with nearby riders and share the delivery location/landmark with the assigned rider.
  • To send order status updates and service notifications.
  • To provide customer support and to keep the service secure and reliable.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as needed to run the service:

  • Restaurants & riders — the order details and delivery location required to fulfill your order.
  • Service providers — Clerk (authentication), Supabase (secure data hosting), and Stripe (payments).
  • Legal — when required by law or to protect users and the platform.

Drivers & couriers

If you sign up as a courier and use the AtlaasGo Driver app, we collect additional information needed to assign jobs, get orders to customers, and pay you. This is separate from the information we collect about customers.

  • Precise location while online — when you toggle yourself online, the Driver app collects your precise (GPS) location continuously so we can match you with nearby orders and route deliveries. This happens only while the app is in the foreground and you are online — we do not track your location in the background or when you are offline. During an active delivery, your live location is shared with the customer so they can follow the order on the map. Location sharing stops when the delivery is complete or when you go offline.
  • Vehicle details — your vehicle make/model and license plate, so customers and support can identify the delivery vehicle.
  • Verification documents — identity and eligibility documents you submit to be approved as a courier.
  • Delivery, earnings & payout information — the deliveries you complete, your earnings, and the payout details needed to pay you (payouts are processed through Stripe).

The in-app delivery destination is set from the customer's device location, so couriers are routed to the customer's actual spot. The app no longer uses manual “landmark” text for this.

Couriers can permanently delete their account and associated data at any time, directly from within the AtlaasGo Driver app.

Data retention & account deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time, directly in the app: Account → Delete account. This removes your profile, addresses, favorites, wallet, and order history. You can also email support@atlaasgo.com to request deletion. Some records may be retained where required for legal, tax, or accounting purposes.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access is restricted with row-level security and authentication controls.

Children

AtlaasGo is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

You may access, correct, or delete your personal data. For any privacy request, contact us at the email below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.

Questions about your privacy? Contact support@atlaasgo.com.
